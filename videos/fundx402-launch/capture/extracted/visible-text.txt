FUND402 + CLAWBACK — 90s product launch (no-capture, dev SDK)

PRODUCT
Fund402 — just-in-time credit + x402 settlement for AI agents on Casper. When an
autonomous agent hits an HTTP 402 paywall with an empty wallet, a liquidity pool fronts
the CEP-18 micropayment and settles it on-chain; the agent repays later. Clawback — agent
payment escrow with AI-adjudicated disputes ("chargebacks for the machine economy"): a
buyer agent's payment is held in escrow against a spec; a good delivery is released, a bad
one is disputed and an AI verifier refunds the buyer. Tagline: "The credit & settlement
layer for the machine economy."
